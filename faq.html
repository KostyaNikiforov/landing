F.A.Q.
Who is this mentoring program for?
Our program is designed for people aged 17-40 who want to build or improve their careers in IT. It is especially useful if you:
  • Want to get your first developer job
  • Feel lost among thousands of courses
  • Need guidance from an experienced developer
  • Want to grow faster in your current IT role
No matter your starting level, we help you create a clear path forward.
Do I need programming experience?
No. We work with students at different levels:
  • Beginners with no experience
  • Self-taught developers
  • Junior developers preparing for their first job
  • Developers who want to improve their skills or change specialization
Your mentor will assess your current level and build a personal roadmap.
How often do we meet with the mentor?
You will have one personal meeting per week with your mentor. During these sessions you will:
  • Review homework and progress
  • Discuss challenges and questions
  • Adjust your learning plan
  • Set goals for the next week
Between meetings, you can contact your mentor in chat during office hours (Mon-Fri, 9:00-18:00).
How long does the program last?
The duration depends on:
  • Your current level
  • Your learning pace
  • Your career goal
Typically, mentoring lasts 3-6 months, but the plan can be shorter or longer depending on your progress.
Will you help me find a job?
Yes — we help you prepare for the hiring process by:
  • Improving your CV
  • Preparing test projects
  • Practicing technical interviews
  • Giving career advice
In some cases we can also communicate with recruiters or HR departments on your behalf.
Is this a course?
No.
Unlike standard courses, this is personal mentoring. That means:
  • No large groups
  • No generic lectures
  • No one-size-fits-all curriculum
Instead, you receive individual guidance and feedback from an experienced developer.
Can I study while working or studying at university?
Yes.
The program is designed to be flexible and remote, so you can combine it with work or studies. Your mentor will help you build a realistic schedule that fits your lifestyle.
How do I start?
Simply leave your name, phone number, and goal in the application form on the landing page. Our manager will contact you and schedule the first meeting. Get free consultation
